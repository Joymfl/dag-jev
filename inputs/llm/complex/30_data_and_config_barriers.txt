0. Create configuration:w:config.toml
1. Create raw A:w:raw_a.csv
2. Create raw B:w:raw_b.csv
3. Normalize A:r:raw_a.csv,config.toml:w:clean_a.csv
4. Normalize B:r:raw_b.csv,config.toml:w:clean_b.csv
5. Archive both raw datasets:r:raw_a.csv,raw_b.csv:w:raw_archive.zip
6. Replace both raw datasets:w:raw_a.csv,raw_b.csv
7. Normalize new A:r:raw_a.csv,config.toml:w:next_a.csv
8. Normalize new B:r:raw_b.csv,config.toml:w:next_b.csv
9. Compare old and new normalized datasets:r:clean_a.csv,clean_b.csv,next_a.csv,next_b.csv:w:comparison.txt
10. Replace configuration:w:config.toml
11. Package comparison with new config and archive:r:comparison.txt,config.toml,raw_archive.zip:w:package.zip
